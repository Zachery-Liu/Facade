# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Facade is an open-source project for people working together across different backgrounds, experience levels, and working styles. Contributions should be discussed with technical clarity, patience, and respect.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes when new evidence appears;
- respecting contributors' time and boundaries;
- helping keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or demeaning language or imagery;
- deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith argument, or targeted spam;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sexualized attention or other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy or materially disrupt the project. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on maintaining a productive technical community.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. If a private reporting channel is not otherwise listed for the repository, contact the repository owner through the contact methods available on their GitHub profile.

When possible, include links, screenshots, or other context needed to understand the incident. Do not include unrelated private information.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository-hosted project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other official collaboration channels, as well as representation of the project in related public spaces.
